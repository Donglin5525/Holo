## 一、版本更新说明 What's New

### 简体中文（zh-Hans）

Holo 1.0.4：不止记下生活，更帮你推进生活。

【进行中的事】旅行、装修、求职……人生每件大事有了家：Holo 帮你拆解成待办、跟进进展、记下每一步；和 Holo 聊聊这件事，它记得全部来龙去脉。

【全新「今天」看板】打开就知道现在最值得做什么——今日安排、进行中的事、习惯与支出状态一页看全；首页光球同步焕新。

【睡眠报告重做】整晚睡眠铺在真实时间轴上：入睡、深睡、做梦、夜醒一目了然，午睡不再混进夜晚。

【习惯「累计」上线】打卡习惯新增全历史累计总账，只增不减，陪伴看得见；打卡瞬间磁贴即时 +1。

【AI 更懂收纳】AI 建的任务自动归入清单（如「日本旅行」），不再散落一地；全部完成时，庆祝卡说对时间的话。

【新手更友好】第一次使用有行动卡引路，人生第一笔记录有小庆祝；各模块空状态一键直达，不用先找按钮。

【记账更顺手】固定支出保存即落账；科目管理与记一笔体验统一；记一笔跟随你选中的日期；相册权限受限时有明确指引。

还有：深度分析报告可继续提问、可生成分享卡；任务详情弹窗、记忆长廊滑动、今日看板点击等一批稳定性修复。

### English (en-US)

Holo 1.0.4: not just recording your life, but helping you move it forward.

[In Progress] Life's big undertakings—travel, renovations, a job search—finally have a home. Holo breaks them into to-dos, tracks progress, and remembers every step. Discuss any of them with Holo, and it recalls the full story.

[New "Today" board] Know what's most worth doing the moment you open the app—today's schedule, matters in progress, habits and spending at a glance. The home orb gets a fresh look too.

[Sleep report, redesigned] A full night laid out on a real timeline: falling asleep, deep sleep, dreams, and night wakings, all clear at a glance. Afternoon naps no longer blur into the night.

[Habit "Total"] A lifetime tally for check-in habits that only ever grows. Watch it tick up the instant you check in.

[Smarter AI organization] Tasks created by AI are automatically grouped into lists (like "Japan Trip"). And when you clear your day, the celebration says the right thing at the right time.

[Friendlier start] A first-step action card guides new users, with a little celebration for your very first entry. Empty states now take you straight to where you need to go.

[Better bookkeeping] Fixed expenses post to your ledger the moment you save; category management matches the add-transaction experience; quick entry follows your selected date; clear guidance when photo access is limited.

Plus: deep-analysis reports you can question and share, and a batch of stability fixes.
