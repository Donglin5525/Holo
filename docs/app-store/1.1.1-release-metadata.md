**描述**

Holo 將記帳、待辦、習慣和靈感筆記放在一起，幫你記錄生活，也幫你回顧生活。

記下一筆消費，整理今天的任務，完成一次習慣打卡，留下突然想到的點子。AI 資料處理授權由你決定；未授權時，記帳、待辦、習慣和筆記等本機功能仍可正常使用。授權後，HoloAI 可以協助整理、分類和總結，讓零散資訊更容易回看。

【記帳與收支統計】
記錄日常收入、支出、消費分類和帳戶，整理帳單與消費記錄，支援帳單週期和專案預算。
回顧錢花在哪裡，為預算安排和存錢計畫提供記錄依據。

【截圖與語音輔助記錄】
將帳單截圖傳送給 HoloAI，協助辨識金額、分類和帳戶；也可以透過語音輸入表達需要記錄的內容。
相關 AI 功能需要開啟 AI 資料處理授權。支援匯出帳目及收據圖片，方便留存與核對。

【待辦清單與時間管理】
整理任務、計畫和行程，用四象限查看重要與緊急程度，再選擇今天要處理的事情。
透過完成記錄與任務統計回顧執行情況，讓計畫清單更容易落實。

【習慣打卡與追蹤】
記錄習慣完成情況，透過日曆和進度回顧堅持的節奏。
將習慣放到桌面小工具，也可以直接完成打卡，讓每天的小行動更容易堅持。

【靈感筆記與日常記錄】
保存想法、觀點、隨筆、標籤和引用。寫下日記片段、備忘事項或一條簡短便箋，讓靈感留得下來。
開啟相關 AI 功能後，可以協助整理主題，方便回看相關筆記。

【日曆與 AI 復盤】
透過週曆、月曆回看每天的收支、任務、習慣和生活記錄。
開啟 AI 資料處理授權後，HoloAI 可以協助總結近期狀態、分析記錄之間的關聯，並把值得回看的線索整理到記憶長廊。

【健康資料與小工具】
授權 Apple Health 後，只讀查看步數、睡眠、站立和運動資料，以及睡眠階段、活動分布和體徵趨勢。
將待辦、習慣和收支放到桌面或鎖定畫面，讓常用資訊更容易看到。

【iCloud 同步與資料管理】
Holo 透過使用者自己的 iCloud 私人資料庫，在裝置間同步本機記錄。App 內提供隱私權政策、使用條款和帳號／資料刪除入口。

AI 產生內容僅供參考，不構成醫療、財務、法律或投資建議。

隱私權與資料：
Holo 不使用第三方廣告追蹤。
Holo 不會主動儲存你傳送給 AI 的原始請求內容、語音音訊或完整上下文作為使用者資料。
為保障服務安全、流量限制和故障排查，Holo 後端會保存最少量技術日誌或摘要資訊，並依後台設定定期清理。
Holo 不會將從 Apple HealthKit 讀取的原始健康資料寫入或同步到 Holo 的 iCloud 資料庫；使用需要健康情境的 AI 功能時，必要的健康摘要會在使用者同意 AI 資料處理後傳送至 Holo 後端和第三方 AI 服務。

**Description**

Holo brings expense tracking, tasks, habits, and idea notes together so you can record your life and look back on it.

Add an expense, organize today’s tasks, check off a habit, or save an idea. AI data processing is optional. Local features such as expenses, tasks, habits, and notes continue to work when it is off. When enabled, HoloAI can help organize, categorize, and summarize your records so they are easier to revisit.

EXPENSE TRACKING AND SPENDING INSIGHTS
Record income, expenses, categories, and accounts. Organize bills and spending records, and set billing cycles and project budgets. See where your money goes and use your records to plan a budget or savings goal.

PHOTO AND VOICE-ASSISTED ENTRY
Send a bill image to HoloAI to help identify the amount, category, and account. You can also describe a record by voice. AI features require AI data processing permission. Export transaction records and receipt images for safekeeping and review.

TASKS AND TIME MANAGEMENT
Organize tasks, plans, and schedules. Use a four-quadrant view to sort tasks by importance and urgency, then choose what to work on today. Review completions and task statistics to see how your plans are progressing.

HABIT TRACKING
Track habit completion and review your consistency in calendar and progress views. Add habits to Home Screen widgets and check them off from a widget, making small daily actions easier to maintain.

IDEA NOTES AND DAILY JOURNALING
Save ideas, opinions, notes, tags, and quotes. Capture a journal entry, reminder, or quick note before it slips away. With the related AI feature enabled, Holo can help organize topics so related notes are easier to find.

CALENDAR AND AI REFLECTION
Use week and month views to review daily expenses, tasks, habits, and life notes. With AI data processing enabled, HoloAI can summarize recent patterns, look for relationships across your records, and save useful insights to Memory Gallery.

HEALTH DATA AND WIDGETS
With Apple Health permission, view steps, sleep, stand hours, and workouts in read-only mode, along with sleep stages, activity distribution, and health trends. Add tasks, habits, and spending to Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets for quick access.

ICLOUD SYNC AND DATA MANAGEMENT
Holo syncs local records between your devices through your own private iCloud database. The app provides access to its Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and account and data deletion tools.

AI-generated content is for reference only and is not medical, financial, legal, or investment advice.

PRIVACY AND DATA
Holo does not use third-party ad tracking.
Holo does not actively store the original request text, voice audio, or complete context you send to AI as user records.
For service security, rate limiting, and troubleshooting, Holo’s backend keeps minimal technical logs or summaries and clears them regularly according to server settings.
Holo does not write raw health data read from Apple HealthKit to its iCloud database. If you use an AI feature that needs health context, a necessary health summary is sent to Holo’s backend and third-party AI services only after you grant AI data processing permission.
