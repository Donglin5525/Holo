# Holo Plus A2：肖像、麦穗与右上角角标

日期：2026-10-29。东林确认稿为本聊天的「A2 · 肖像麦穗」。

## 最终形态

- 沿用 A 的流畅右向肖像，两侧为麦穗，PLUS 位于右上角。
- 个人页、会员中心、升级页共用 `HoloPlusEmblem`。2026-10-30 按东林反馈将个人页入口收为扁横条：徽章 56pt、上下留白各 12pt，常规字号下高度约 80pt；会员中心、升级页保持 72pt。
- 个人页文案由三行收为「Holo Plus + 会员状态」两行，进入箭头独立放在右侧；辅助功能大字体允许自然增高。
- 免费态展示同系列银灰肖像；Plus 态展示金色肖像、麦穗和原生 PLUS 角标。权益状态继续来自既有订阅服务。
- 所有内容限制在声明的正方形内；36pt 小尺寸兼容已有宿主，不横向挤占文案。

## 正式资源与呈现

- `Holo/Assets.xcassets/HoloPlusPortraitMask.imageset/`：免费肖像。
- `Holo/Assets.xcassets/HoloPlusPortraitWheatMask.imageset/`：会员肖像与麦穗。
- 两份素材均由内置 imagegen 基于确认稿制作。纯黑底、白色线稿保留平滑边缘，组件使用亮度蒙版把黑底转成透明，再应用 `HoloPlusTheme.emblemGoldGradient` / `emblemSilverGradient`。
- 生成透明稿时出现了边缘杂点，正式资源采用干净的黑白亮度稿；原生渲染中轻微提高对比度消除近黑底色。
- PLUS 使用原生文字和胶囊绘制，随图形尺寸计算宽高，保持单行与清晰边缘。
- 既有 `HoloFaceLineArt` 及 App 图标保留，原始稿与其他业务图标不受替换。

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Produce a clean production luminosity-mask artwork for native iOS rendering. Redraw the approved A2 portrait and wheat emblem from the reference as CRISP PURE WHITE vector-style lines and white filled wheat grains, centered on a PERFECTLY SOLID PURE BLACK square background. OPAQUE IMAGE, NO TRANSPARENCY, no alpha cutout. Exactly one icon only. Preserve the approved right-facing Holo facial silhouette, smooth flowing curves, and two symmetrical ears of wheat. NO PLUS tag because native text will be overlaid separately. No text, no card, no rounded square, no headings, no miniatures, no colored pixels, no lighting, no gray fog, no shadows, no texture, no speckles. Pure black background and smooth antialiased pure white geometry only. At 1024 square, use about 12px continuous strokes with rounded caps, not outlined double strokes. Fill wheat grains white. Leave 6% black padding on all sides. Face starts about 12% down the canvas, wheat open above; reserve top right for a native badge. This is graphic vector-style artwork, not a photograph and not an automatic background-removal operation.
```

No wheat, no wreath, no PLUS, no text, no card, no rounded square. No color, no gray fog, no texture, no lighting, no speckles. Only smooth antialiased white strokes, about 12px at 1024 square, with rounded caps and the original graceful silhouette.
